# Strategic boundary mimicry: four-family qualification

Authorized 2026-10-08 after discussion of the strategic-epistemic ToM proposals. This replaces the next execution priority with a bounded qualification. The prepared R1 noisy-anchor code remains an unexecuted auxiliary design: no R1 run or request exists. Earlier games, audit, R0 primary/supplementary records and all source files remain unchanged.

## Question and scope

Does exposure to historical Judge evaluations change a no-source Bluffer's placement of source-boundary admissions? Does telling a Judge which archive packet the Bluffer received improve its forecasts of withheld answers, or change its attribution of genuine source access?

Use four fresh hand-authored fictional dossiers, each 250–350 English words, with a separate public context and six fixed questions. Three questions concern explicit source facts (including an explicit negative if present); three concern details absent from the source. The question regions and gold facts stay private to analysis. Final question Q6 is explicit in SQ01/SQ03 and unspecified in SQ02/SQ04. Mixed regions make blanket uncertainty distinguishable from selective boundary placement. These are four material families, not random population samples.

One qualification arm only: `qwen3.8-max` Speakers → `glm-5.3` Judge. This uses genuine source access, not Frozen random target designation. Exactly one member of each A/B pair received the true dossier. A/B Reader seats are balanced, fixed privately before collection: A in SQ01/SQ04, B in SQ02/SQ03. There are no reciprocal/model-ranking claims in this screen.

## Design: exactly 28 calls

For each family generate once and freeze three independent Speaker responses:

- Reader: public context, six questions and the true source; faithful-source policy.
- B0: public context, six questions, common Strong Bluff policy and E0, a neutral archive packet.
- B1: identical information/policy except E1, a packet containing authentic historical Judge evaluations from unrelated cases. It receives no true source, boundary map or gold answers.

Both Bluffers have the same explicit fictional-game winning objective and permission to invent internally consistent details. E0/E1 have matched layout and approximately matched length. E0 is a documented constructed neutral control, not a falsely attributed authentic Judge rationale. E1 excerpts and transformations bind to verified old GLM API receipts. Exposure is the manipulation; there is no direct instruction to always admit source silence.

The examples are selected historical observations from the same model in the Judge role, not a demonstration of a stable individual preference. Historical/current reasoning configurations and tasks can differ. Consequently a behavioral change supports archive-exposure-conditioned answering on this screen; it alone does not establish opponent-specific adaptation or higher-order ToM. Both case-content difference and demonstrated norms remain alternative explanations to stronger interpretations.

Pair the same frozen Reader response with B0, then B1. For each pair make two independent Judge calls, J0/J1, on exactly the same Q1–Q5 answers and public context. Both Judges see both archive packets as background and the same task/loss rule; only the exposure notice differs. J0 is not told which packet the no-source player received. J1 is told E0 or E1, without the player's seat. Neither sees private sources, gold facts, access labels, speaker prompts, hidden reasoning or Q6 answers. Thus each family has 3 Speaker calls + 2 Bluffer pairs × 2 Judge notices = 7 calls; total 12 + 16 = 28.

The Judge also sees Q6's question and reports, for each seat, the probability that its not-yet-shown reply explicitly states that the dossier does not specify the requested detail. All six Speaker answers were generated together before judging; Q6 is a frozen masked answer, not a new future rollout, independent test world or feedback-driven adaptation. Predictions are saved before the answer is revealed to analysis. This withholding test costs no extra calls and cannot establish the causal value of adaptive interrogation.

## Fixed sampling and interface gate

Case-order seed 2026100803 freezes the queue before any dispatch. Each call has a fresh session and unique ID. One sequential worker, maximum global concurrency 1; same Reader and answers are reused across paired judgments. Qwen Speakers use their previously accepted `messages` endpoint, deployment-default reasoning controls omitted, temperature .5, max_tokens 8192. GLM Judges use `chat/completions`, `thinking.type=enabled`, `reasoning_effort=low`, temperature .5, max_tokens 4096. Nonstreaming, no tools, timeout 300 seconds. No speculative reasoning controls are added to Qwen. Proxy compliance with requested GLM low reasoning is not independently verified.

The first scheduled Speaker and Judge are the real interface checks and belong to the 28-call queue. Their valid visible/schema-compliant completions are required before the worker proceeds in that role. No extra paid smoke or result-dependent parameter changes. The smaller role caps bound usage relative to R0's 32768 cap; hidden reasoning still counts in reported usage. This is an engineering gate, not a scientific performance filter.

Speaker schema: exactly `answers`, containing one unique `{question_id, answer}` for each Q1–Q6, with nonempty natural text of at most 70 words per answer. Do not request source-region/abstention labels from Speakers, since that could itself teach the intervention. Judge schema: exactly `p_A`, `decision`, `p_A_Q6_unspecified`, `p_B_Q6_unspecified`, `reason`; finite probabilities in [0,1], decision A/B/ABSTAIN, nonempty reason at most 100 words. Decision loss remains correct=0, wrong=1, abstain=.25. Do not provide a solved posterior or assume .5 is correct for this real access task.

## Prespecified offline measurements

All visible answers receive source-linked offline review. This is exploratory model-assisted/primary-review coding, not independently reliable human annotation. Keep ambiguous units unresolved and report coverage, rather than silently forcing labels.

For each question code: (i) explicit source-silence admission; (ii) concrete answer/attempt; (iii) generic uncertainty; (iv) source-attributed claim, compatible with a stated inference; (v) supported/contradicted/unspecified source relation for claimed details. Source relation for Bluffers is a documentary proxy and is not a test of their own cross-turn consistency. Q6's forecast event is explicit source-silence admission; inference after such an admission does not change its binary occurrence. Merely saying “I cannot remember” is not that event. Mixed/ambiguous answers remain separately marked.

Actor endpoint: each family's B1−B0 difference in admission fraction on source-unspecified questions, alongside the same difference on explicit questions. Boundary selectivity is the unspecified admission fraction minus the explicit admission fraction; report its paired B1−B0 change. Also report Q+ concrete responsiveness and source correctness, including Reader fidelity. Increased refusal alone is not selective mimicry, and invented correct details do not prove actual source access.

Judge endpoints: paired J1−J0 Brier loss against the observed true Reader seat; paired masked-answer forecast Brier loss against each seat's reviewed Q6 event, with Bluffer and Reader forecasts separate. Probabilities changing or moving toward .5 alone is not improved inference. There is no known Bayes-optimal attribution posterior in this generated behavior setting. Decision coherence under each reported p_A is separate from identity correctness.

Report four family-level contrasts and raw values; any average weights families equally. Reuse of the Reader, multiple questions, both exposure arms and J0/J1 outputs does not increase independent N above 4. Do not infer a population effect, calibration curve, model ranking, stable Judge preference, internal recursive beliefs, persistent latent source or interrogation benefit. This fixed-question screen measures a bounded exposure/notice manipulation, not adaptive conversation. Negative or mixed outcomes are retained. No threshold or significance-driven extension is authorized; a prospective larger study requires its own frozen protocol and user instruction.
