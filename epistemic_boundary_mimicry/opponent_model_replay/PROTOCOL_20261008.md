Bind outcomes to SQ28's existing exact-text coding. These are inherited exploratory primary-review labels, not independent human annotation. There are 48 target answer events: B0 24 clear zeros; B1 23 clear zeros and unresolved SQ02/B1/Q6. Preserve that null. For each repeat compute mean forecast, mean signed error `p−y`, and mean Brier `(p−y)^2` over six events, with missing-label coverage explicit. Within a family-condition first average repetitions for each event's probability; then average event-level signed errors and **mean per-repeat Brier losses** over questions. Average these summaries equally across the four families. Also report Brier of the mean probability separately: it is a forecast ensemble, not the same as mean single-call risk. Primary six-question Brier contrasts require complete labels, so complete-case B1 and paired B1−B0 means have three of four families; show all four under both sensitivity resolutions. Available five-question SQ02 results are separate. Report complete-case source-explicit/unspecified strata with their own coverage; no ambiguous event is silently treated as zero.

Report B1−B0 changes in forecast probabilities and observed admission fractions alongside Brier differences; these changes describe forecast sensitivity to prompt variants, not an estimated causal effect on Qwen behavior. Record repeat dispersion as Judge sampling variability only. Repeated forecasts share the same frozen target answers; they are not independent actor outcomes. There are four authored material families, not four random population draws or 16 independent calls, 96 independent predictions or 48 independent answer samples.

There is only one previously collected Speaker draw per family-condition. Its binary outcome is a realized event, **not a known true conditional behavior probability**. Therefore a forecast above zero is not automatically irrational or proof of a calibrated-probability violation. The same families and hypothesis were chosen after inspecting SQ28, so this is hypothesis-guided retrospective replay, not held-out confirmation. Predicted-positive mass or positive signed error can describe overprediction on these frozen outputs; they do not prove population calibration, a stable strategic prior, an internal opponent model, or a causal explanation of SQ03's identity error. Low forecasts would weaken this particular explanation, without validating alternative mechanisms. No result-dependent thresholds, prompt changes, extra repeats or sample expansion.
