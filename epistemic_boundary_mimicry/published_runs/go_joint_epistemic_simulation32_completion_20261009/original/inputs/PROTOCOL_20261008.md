# JES32: known-mechanism joint-response qualification

Authorized 2026-10-08 after the CSB30 interpretation discussion. This is a small static Judge qualification before any naturalistic strategic experiment. No old record is audited or extended, and no new LLM Reader or Bluffer is generated. The simulated response mechanisms are host-defined; this does not measure spontaneous deception, strategic adaptation or Theory of Mind as a general capability.

## Question and exact generating process

Can a Judge use cross-question dependence when both participants have identical single-question marginals, and report uncertainty when their full observed response laws coincide?

Use one fictional harbor-audit setting and two fixed questions about morning/evening stamp entries. Stamp values are copper/slate, encoded internally as 0/1. Both simulated participants use exactly the same deterministic answer renderer. A fair coin assigns the Reader to A or B. Independently, its finite source records either copper/copper or slate/slate with equal probability. The Reader accurately reports both entries. The Judge knows the mechanism but sees neither source nor coin outcomes.

The non-reader is independent of the Reader's source and assignment, and follows one disclosed mechanism:

- MARGINAL: two independent fair coins choose the two stamp answers. Each single-question law matches the Reader's.
- JOINT: one independent fair coin chooses a stamp, then that same stamp is used for both answers. Its joint response law matches the Reader's.

The Judge is explicitly told these generation rules are complied with, replies are uniformly formatted, no other clue is available, and prior seats are equiprobable. Only the relevant mechanism's prose description is shown. Internal condition/packet IDs, vectors, likelihoods, posterior, weights, other packets and aggregate optimal scores are withheld. No numerical Bayes solution or worked example is supplied.

This knowledge is necessary for a known-posterior qualification. It is not the instruction a naturalistic Judge would ordinarily receive. Exactly matching two-answer laws establishes equivalence only for this fixed observation, not arbitrary adaptive interrogation.

## Finite experiment and proof

For a two-answer vector y, define R(00)=R(11)=1/2 and R(01)=R(10)=0. Let B_M(y)=1/4 for all four vectors and B_J=R. Under Reader seat A, an ordered packet h=(a,b) has likelihood L_A=R(a)B(b); under seat B, L_B=B(a)R(b). The fair prior gives predictive weight w(h)=(L_A+L_B)/2 and posterior p*(h)=L_A/(L_A+L_B) whenever the denominator is positive.

MARGINAL has 12 positive-support packets: four with two internally equal vectors have weight 1/8 each and posterior 1/2; eight with exactly one unequal vector have weight 1/16 each and posterior 0 or 1. Both unequal is impossible and is excluded before sampling. JOINT has four positive-support packets, each weight 1/4 and posterior 1/2. Each condition's weights sum to one.

Under MARGINAL, half the predictive mass is diagnostically certain and half ambiguous. Optimal forced identity accuracy is therefore 3/4, not a per-packet posterior of .75. Under JOINT it is 1/2. Optimal expected Brier is .125 and .25 respectively. These are exact finite calculations under the supplied mechanisms, not empirical estimates of LLM Actor laws.

Enumerate every positive-support packet once for each of two Judge models: (12+4) x 2 = **32 one-shot calls**. This is an exhaustive, nonuniformly weighted finite stimulus set, not an iid draw of 32 games or 16 independent source families. Repeated answer vectors across conditions remain different mechanism-conditioned stimuli. One request per model/cell does not estimate call variance.

Seed 2026100807 shuffles all 32 requests; each uses a fresh session. There is no Speaker generation, follow-up, alternative phrasing, repetition or naturalistic continuation in this launch. The first scheduled actual request is the interface/schema check and counts within the 32.

## Reports, scoring and qualification gate

Output strict JSON with exactly p_A, decision and reason. Probability must be finite in [0,1]; decision A/B/ABSTAIN; nonempty reason at most 80 English words. Reject duplicated keys, code fences, incomplete output and substantive repairs. Reparse the exact visible text independently for analysis.

The primary endpoint, separately for each model/condition, is predictive-weighted posterior squared error sum_h w(h)(p_hat(h)-p*(h))^2. The decomposition of expected Brier is this excess plus sum_h w(h)p*(h)(1-p*(h)); hence wrong-confidence and correct ambiguity are not conflated.

Record loss correct=0, wrong=1, abstain=.25. The corresponding Bayes action is A if posterior>.75, B if posterior<.25 and ABSTAIN otherwise; ties abstain. Expected decision loss uses the known conditional identity posterior, rather than randomly attaching a realized identity to ambiguous packets. Report its excess over Bayes loss, weighted coverage, posterior error on ambiguous packets, diagnostic direction, maximum error and forced-pick expected accuracy. Forced-pick scores use .5 correctness at a reported tie and do not treat abstention as a wrong identity choice. Ambiguous normatively justified abstention is not a failure.

An operational gate passes only if all 32 responses are valid and each of the four model/condition primary errors is at most .01. This prespecified tolerance is a practical screen, not a significance threshold or calibrated reliability guarantee. No automatic next-stage launch follows. Incomplete conditions have no renormalized primary score or pass; report retained predictive mass, all failures and undispatched cells.

Rationales are preserved as descriptive evidence; no counted psychological-mechanism taxonomy is planned. Results characterize one known finite mechanism with a fixed renderer, not general ToM, model ranking, naturalistic bluffing, LLM joint simulation, source-distribution learning or adaptive questioning.
