Question: do Judges select or abstain when two finite sources share a public domain but no longer use parallel sentence substitution?

They share a fact-slot skeleton to roughly match information density, but differ in phrasing, organization and details. This is not a controlled causal comparison with the older template cases.

Each background runs Qwen3.8 Max readers → GLM-5.3 Judge and the reciprocal direction, in fresh independent role sessions. Four trajectories share two material families, the independent descriptive units. No additional models, Strong conditions or perturbation tests are launched.

Freeze all source texts and hashes first; randomize and freeze seats separately per trajectory; draw independent target labels only afterward. Source documents and prompts do not depend on targets. The control concerns arbitrary target recovery, not detection of which agent has knowledge.
