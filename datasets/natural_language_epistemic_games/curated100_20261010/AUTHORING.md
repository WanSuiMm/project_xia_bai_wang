The user selected a total of 100 distinct natural-language source worlds on 2026-10-10, including eligible legacy worlds and the existing 24 development worlds. This is material construction, not authorization for provider calls or publication. Legacy dialogue endpoints remain heterogeneous exploratory evidence; newly authored sources are not collected dialogues.

## New sources

Write assigned `new_worlds/NLxxx.json` files using the schema in `../dev24_20261010/AUTHORING.md`, with `split: "candidate_test"`. Each source has 350–550 English words in coherent prose and a 40–100-word public context. At least 5 supported claims, 2 explicit negatives, 2 bounded inferences, 3 genuinely unspecified probes and 2 multi-fact consistency questions are required. Evidence covers every clause of a supported claim and is a literal source substring. All references are local claim IDs.

The content must differ in substantive relations, events, procedures and source genre, not merely replace named entities or numbers. The existing 24 worlds cover seed reserves, acoustic detections, nutrient trials, laminates, optical coupons, mechanical joints, stratigraphy, catalog transfers, painted-stone treatment, shade queues, street-use trials, fare appeals, tool lending, shared meals, repair consent, discourse particles, conversational repair, quotation scope, graph routes, partial orders, seven-peg incidence, warning times, seal fees and bundle custody. Do not clone these factual skeletons.

## Diversity and boundaries

- Write distinct genres: meeting notes, instructions, correspondence, oral-history excerpts, incident reports, case narratives, observational diaries and technical records. Vary perspective, paragraph organization and location of concrete details. Do not make every source a study concluding that more evidence is needed.
- Give the source a substantive account: events, linked procedures, social obligations, mechanisms, decisions, results or conflicting attributed viewpoints. Some facts must resolve something. Reader access should reveal more than a uniformly cautious writing style.
- Place explicit negatives naturally. Do not end with a checklist of absent measurements or unknowns. Leave consequential relations or outcomes naturally unreported.
- At least one unspecified probe concerns a consequential unresolved relationship, exception, mechanism, decision or outcome. A candidate already ruled out or established by the source is not unspecified.
- Distinguish an explicit nonoccurrence, an explicit refusal to infer, and silence about an event. Preserve attributed testimony as testimony. Inferences must not become unsupported causal or universal claims.
- Public context supplies only setting, document genre and broad familiar ontology, without private results, exact procedures or source-boundary hints. Names used for setting are permissible; private numerical results are not.
- No Boolean tables, probability exercises, real personal records, credentials, URLs to accounts or machine paths. Mathematics/linguistics may appear as ordinary documents, but do not replace the overall corpus with puzzles.

## Provenance and grouping

Assign one family ID per distinct factual skeleton, document related skeletons honestly, and preserve all variants under one source world. Unique IDs, separate writing sessions and low text similarity do not prove independence. Candidate-test designation only means no response-dependent development has used the material yet; actual test release requires a frozen protocol and content hashes before collection.

Only write assigned files. Do not edit old evidence, configuration, other agents' worlds or final manifests. No API calls or GitHub push.
