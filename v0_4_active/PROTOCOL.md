These are fresh synthetic binary bench-fixture cases, not reused published prompts. Six independent cases, three conditional judge replicates each (18 trajectories, not 18 independent backgrounds).

Each speaker generates once: 80–120-word rule account plus eight frozen PASS/FAIL predictions. The judge initially sees only accounts and eight inputs, never the prediction table, private card, seat label or gold. On VERIFY, the host reveals the two precommitted predictions and code-computed truth, then asks for the next action. No additional speaker calls or commitment revision. This implements live revelation of frozen answers, not strategic adaptation during interrogation. Two speakers use the same model and format. Judges have independent chats; updates stay in the same chat. Choices: VERIFY unused Q, STOP with A/B, UNIDENTIFIABLE with null pick. Cap eight checks; one final decision after the eighth is allowed.

Four cases target observable disagreement, but unknown guesses can collide and known execution can fail. Actual diagnostic queries are computed from frozen predictions, not intended labels. Audit known predictions against deterministic gold. Keep collisions, errors and malformed outputs; do not regenerate or select replacement cases to achieve 4/2. The two negative controls deliberately publish the standard-mode rule and restrict all eight queries to that mode; their hidden enhanced-mode details are unverifiable. They are simple verification-channel controls, not evidence that full utterances contain no access cues. No general impossibility or knowledge-access proof follows from prediction equality.

Scientific question: selecting useful verification, using revealed evidence and stopping. Primary metrics: first-query disagreement (premature stopping separately), correct-side decision/update after a discriminating observation (direction auxiliary with probability saturation handled), and verification cost/extra queries after first distinguishing truth. Final accuracy reported for known-correct, actually distinguishable cases; non-identifiable abstention and needless cost separately. Matching one outcome is not proof of private access; empirical attribution is scored against randomized access labels under this controlled instruction regime. Do not count a platform missing response as model error. Case-level counts plus conditional replicate variation; no population/causal/ToM claim or model ranking.

Deterministic baseline: given the frozen prediction table, choose any disagreement, reveal gold, pick the sole matching speaker; if no disagreement, abstain. It is an oracle-table cost lower bound (1 versus 0), not an equal-information policy: the actual judge must infer disagreement from accounts. Report this distinction.
