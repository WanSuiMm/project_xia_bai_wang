# Naturalistic Study 1 — live collection protocol

The user authorized live Arena collection on 2026-10-03. Twelve locally authored fresh fictional dossiers are used, each in two reciprocal cross-family configurations, for 24 planned trajectories. UI-observed labels are `claude-sonnet-5-high`, `gpt-5.5-instant`, and `gemini-3.8-flash-high`; these labels are platform identities, not independently verified model versions. H2 remains paused.

Cases N01/N04/N07/N10 use Claude ↔ GPT, N02/N05/N08/N11 Claude ↔ Gemini, and N03/N06/N09/N12 GPT ↔ Gemini. Each direction uses fresh A/B speaker sessions and a fresh judge session. Speaker models match within each pair. Initial informed seats are assigned using seed 2026100305, with opposite seats in reciprocal directions (12 A and 12 B). Speakers are generated once per trajectory and retain their isolated conversations for follow-ups. There is no repeated judge or self-model condition.

Only the Knower receives `source_title` and `source_text`; all three receive the exact public context. Analyst notes are excluded from all tested-role prompts. The Bluffer receives a consensual fictional performance task, no source summary. The Judge sees exact opening/follow-up reply text, without operational role prompts, access labels, model identity, analyst notes, or source. Speaker responses to questions stay in their original independent sessions. The Judge can quote prior dialogue to another speaker, but there is no automatic speaker-to-speaker relay.

The Judge chooses A, B, or BOTH and an unrestricted natural-language question, stops with a pick/confidence/reason, or abstains. Ten ASK actions is a safety cap, not a required length. BOTH counts as one ASK action and produces two answers. At the cap the Judge is instructed to STOP or ABSTAIN. JSON is a routing wrapper only. Host messages relay answers without truth, correction, evaluation, or suggested strategies. The original source and protocol are frozen before the first send; local run `runs/arena_20261003_naturalistic01/` contains the exact bundle and its hash.

Record prompts before submission, actual visible model/mode, UTC timestamps, raw final replies, question targets, terminal choices/confidence, and missing/protocol failures. Never retry for answer quality. An invalid response ends the affected trajectory as a protocol failure. Platform errors are missing, not incorrect judgments. After approximately five minutes without output, allow one reload for recovery; preserve the cutoff if still unavailable, without resending. Three consecutive platform failures stop the affected collection batch. Human security challenges are handed to the user. Anonymous comparisons must be skipped and the selected fixed model verified before capturing; unresolved identity ends the trajectory.

First operational batch: N01/N04/N07/N10. Continue the remaining planned cases after the routing smoke works, without modifying questions or sources based on outcomes. This is an exploratory qualitative study. Report terminal accuracy among completed picks, abstentions, asks, and missingness. Inspect recurring interaction patterns using quoted transcript evidence after collection, without treating a post-hoc taxonomy as preregistered. Source consistency is distinct from initial-access identification.

There is no passive baseline, human baseline, matched token budget, self-model comparison, or matched actor/observer test. Thus no interaction improvement, token-independent causal effect, actor–observer dissociation, family ranking, or self/cross difference is identified. A small cross-case pattern motivates a later controlled experiment; it does not establish a population mechanism.
